See how it works ↓ For businesses in Croatia

One receipt, three stops before it's filed.

Capture on the phone. Review across the company. Export for accounting.

  1. 01

    Scan and check

    Photograph a receipt. Evido reads its text on-device and extracts fiscal identifiers from the QR code. Check the merchant, date, and amounts before saving. Without a connection, scans wait on the phone until they can upload.

  2. 02

    Review the whole month

    Owners see the team’s receipts in one place, with monthly totals and filters by worker, company, or status. If a scan is unreadable, send it back to the worker with a note.

  3. 03

    Send it to accounting

    Download a PDF or a monthly ZIP organized by worker. UBL 2.1 XML is included when the merchant’s tax ID passes validation. These exports are generated from scanned receipt data, so review them before importing into accounting software.

See it from both sides.

The actual app, in Croatian. Switch between the worker’s receipt list and the owner’s monthly overview.

Worker: this month's receipts

Every receipt the worker has scanned this month, with its status: queued, submitted, or sent back for a rescan by the owner. Scanning works without a signal; queued receipts upload automatically once the phone reconnects.

Svi means all receipts. Za ponoviti lists scans that need another attempt.

Owner: the whole team's month

Every worker's receipts in one monthly view: count, total, and a filter by worker, company, or status. A third count flags the receipts the owner sent back for a rescan.

Broj računa is the receipt count. Ukupno is the month’s total.
