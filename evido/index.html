Built around the R1 receipt.

Snap a photo, done

Point the camera at a receipt. Evido reads the date, merchant, VAT ID (OIB), amount, VAT, and the fiscal codes (JIR/ZKI) — no retyping.

Works without signal

Scans queue on the device and send the moment the connection returns, so nothing is lost on a job site with no reception.

One overview for every worker

Owners see every receipt across the whole team, by month, filterable by worker, company, or status — and can flag one for a rescan.

Ready for the accountant

Every receipt exports as a UBL 2.1 e-invoice (EN 16931) alongside its PDF — one at a time, or the whole month as a single ZIP.

Where it stands

Evido is pre-launch, built for businesses in Croatia. Company data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt); there are no ads and no third-party tracking.
