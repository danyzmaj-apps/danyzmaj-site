Privacy policy

Effective 15 September 2026

Data controller: Danijel Lovrić, contact danijel@danyzmaj.com.

What Evido collects

Evido is built for businesses in Croatia to digitize R1 receipts. Owners sign in with email and password; workers don't have their own email address — an owner creates their access inside the app. What's collected:

DataPurpose
Email address (owners)Sign-in and account management
NameShowing who scanned a receipt
Receipt photos (camera or gallery)Core function — reading receipt data
Receipt data (merchant, date, amounts, VAT, OIB, JIR/ZKI)Core function — building digital and e-invoice records
Company details (name, OIB, address)Grouping receipts and workers under the right company

Evido does not collect location, contacts, device identifiers, or diagnostic data.

Legal basis and purpose

Data is processed to maintain a company's R1 receipt records and produce PDFs and UBL e-invoices for accounting, under the performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6.1.b).

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase Inc. in an EU data center (Frankfurt). Access is restricted to the company the data belongs to and its own workers — one company cannot see another's receipts.

Sharing

Data is not sold and is not shared with third parties. Evido contains no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics tools.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as a company uses Evido. To request deletion of an account or of all of a company's data, email danijel@danyzmaj.com; requests are handled within 30 days.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restricted processing, or object to processing by emailing danijel@danyzmaj.com. If you're in Croatia, you may also complain to AZOP, the Croatian data protection authority; if you're elsewhere in the EU, your local supervisory authority.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when Evido's data practices change, with a revised effective date.
