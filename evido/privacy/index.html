Privacy policy

Effective 15 September 2026

Data controller: Danijel Lovrić, contact danijel@danyzmaj.com.

What Evido collects

Evido is built for businesses in Croatia to digitize R1 receipts. Owners sign in with email and password; workers don't have their own email address. An owner or admin creates their account inside the app, with a system-generated placeholder address used only to satisfy the login system. What's collected:

DataPurpose
Email address (owners)Sign-in and account management
NameShowing who scanned a receipt
Receipt photos (camera or gallery)Core function. Reading receipt data
Receipt data (merchant, date, amounts, VAT, OIB, JIR/ZKI)Core function. Building digital records and e-invoices
Company details (name, OIB, address)Grouping receipts and workers under the right company

Text recognition runs on your device using Google ML Kit before the receipt photo is uploaded. Receipt capture does not need your location or contacts. Infrastructure and SDK providers may process technical request data under their own privacy terms.

Legal basis and purpose

Data is processed to maintain a company's R1 receipt records and produce PDFs and, where a receipt's merchant tax ID reads validly, UBL 2.1 e-invoices for accounting, under the performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6.1.b).

Where data is stored and who processes it

Receipt photos, extracted receipt data, and account records are stored in a Supabase project hosted in Supabase's EU data center (Frankfurt); Supabase Inc. is a data processor acting on these instructions, not an independent recipient of the data. Access to a company's records is restricted by row-level database rules to that company's own owner and workers, plus the service administrator for support and maintenance. One company cannot see another's receipts.

The owner-facing web dashboard is hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel's infrastructure processes visiting IP addresses and request logs as part of serving the page; this website (the one you're reading) is hosted on GitHub Pages, which does the same for its visitors. Neither is Evido-operated tracking.

Sharing

Data is not sold. Evido uses infrastructure providers to store records and operate the service. An owner can export records for their accountant or other recipients they choose. Evido does not add advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Retention and deletion

Receipt records are retained to provide the company's record-keeping service. To request deletion of an account or a company's data, email danijel@danyzmaj.com. The company may have separate accounting or legal obligations to retain records, including copies it has exported.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, your email address, message, and any attachments are used to respond and investigate the issue. The email provider also processes that correspondence. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information in a support request.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restricted processing, or object to processing by emailing danijel@danyzmaj.com. If you're in Croatia, you may also complain to AZOP, the Croatian data protection authority; if you're elsewhere in the EU, your local supervisory authority.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when Evido's data practices change, with a revised effective date.
