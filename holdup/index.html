How it works

Signs, presets
and a clock.

Put a name on screen for an airport pickup, find friends in a crowd, or send a message through a window.

01

Text that fits the screen

Type directly on the sign. Hold Up sizes the text to fit and picks a readable text color for each of its six backgrounds.

02

Save signs for later

Save messages as presets and reopen them in two taps. Useful for names, directions, or anything you show often.

03

Keep the sign visible

Your screen stays awake while a sign is open. You can also show a fullscreen clock or make a message pulse.
