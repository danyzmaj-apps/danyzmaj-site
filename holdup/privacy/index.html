Hold Up privacy policy

Effective 25 August 2026

Hold Up does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app makes no network connections. Nothing you type ever leaves your device.
  • Signs and presets are stored only in the app's local storage on your device, and are removed when you delete the app.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app requests no permissions. It keeps the screen awake while a sign is showing; that happens on-device.
