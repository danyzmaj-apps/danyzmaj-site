Will it work on my Garmin?

PixelPup is built for supported round AMOLED Garmin watches, including models in the Forerunner, Venu, epix, fēnix, and vívoactive families. MIP-display watches aren’t supported. Send me your model if you’re not sure.

How do I make it mine?

After installation, open PixelPup in the Connect IQ app and go to Settings. Choose your breed, palette, light or dark mode, and the stats for your four configurable rows. On touchscreen watches, touch and hold the dog to switch between light and dark within your chosen palette.

Does it collect my data?

PixelPup does not collect your personal data or require a separate account. The watch face displays data from your Garmin. Temperature uses Garmin weather data, refreshed when your watch syncs with your phone. This website uses no analytics or cookies.

How do I get PixelPup?

PixelPup is currently in beta. Coming soon to Garmin Connect IQ.

When it launches, you’ll find a direct store link here. Open the listing in the Connect IQ app, check compatibility with your paired Garmin, and install the watch face. Sync your watch, then select PixelPup as your watch face.
