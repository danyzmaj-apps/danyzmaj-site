      The rules in short

      • One vote per person per month. You can change it as often as you like until the month closes.
      • Each month closes at midnight UTC on the 1st, when the votes are counted. If two breeds tie, the one that reached its total first wins.
      • The winning breed is drawn in pixel art and joins PixelPup in the next update, then leaves the list.
      • The full list leaves out the 20 breeds already in PixelPup.

      Your privacy

      Voting stores a random ID in this browser so you can change your vote later. Your network is checked with a scrambled version of your IP address that is deleted when the month is counted, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks for bots.
