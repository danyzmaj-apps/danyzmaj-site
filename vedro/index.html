Ride ahead of the weather.

vedro reads the sky along your route before you leave the driveway. Every stretch of road is scored by weather severity, so you know exactly where it turns rough — and can pick a better time to go.

See the ride ahead.

vedro breaks your route into segments and colors each one by forecast severity — the same fixed palette used across the app, so a glance at the map tells you what a glance at the sky can't.

Built for riding, not just driving.

Every mile, scored

Your route is sampled point by point and colored by conditions — clear, caution, severe, or no data — so trouble shows up on the map before it shows up on the road.

Pick your moment

Compare departure times side by side. Leaving in twenty minutes can put you ahead of the worst of it, or well behind it.

Speaks up when it matters

On-device voice guidance and a push alert if conditions turn worse than forecast mid-ride, so you don't need to stare at the screen.

No roof, no problem

vedro accounts for your vehicle and heading — riding a motorcycle or top-down convertible feels weather differently than a sealed cabin, and the routing does too.

Where it stands

vedro is currently in TestFlight and Google Play internal testing ahead of a public launch. No account needed — just install and go.
